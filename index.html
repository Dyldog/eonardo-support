Make art out of emoji, one tiny square at a time.

Getting started

  1. In My Pictures, select New Picture.
  2. Choose an emoji in the palette, then tap or drag across the canvas.
  3. Select Add emojis to expand your palette.
  4. Use Grid to change the canvas size while preserving the edge that matters.
  5. Select Copy and paste your emoji artwork into a message.

Pictures save automatically in your local library.

Need help?

Include your device model, system version, and a short description of what happened. Please do not include private artwork unless it is necessary to explain the issue.
